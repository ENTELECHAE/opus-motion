# Opus-Motion: Styles & Prompts Catalog

A specialized skill for picking, customizing, and generating prompts across 59 distinct code film styles and 703 viral video patterns.

## 🎨 Style Categories

### 1. Hand-drawn & Painting
- **`watercolor`** (Watercolor Brush): Wet edges, running pigments, paper texture bleed.
- **`ink-wash`** (Chinese Ink Wash): Flowing brushstrokes, calligraphic lines, negative space.
- **`crayon-book`** (Crayon Picture Book): Heavy grain, waxy streaks, childbook nostalgia.
- **`impasto`** (Impasto Oil Painting): Thick textured strokes, palette knife ridges, warm glow.
- **`one-line`** (One-Line Drawing): Continuous stroke that never leaves the page.

### 2. East Asian Traditions
- **`shadow-puppet`** (Shadow Puppetry): Translucent colored leather, backlit screen, rod joints.
- **`ukiyoe`** (Ukiyo-e Woodblock): Flowing waves, wood grain transfer, flat color bands.
- **`papercut-red`** (Red Paper-Cut): Ornate scissors geometry, positive-negative paper symmetry.
- **`paper-lantern`** (Paper-Cut Lightbox): Layered shadowbox depth, warm internal illumination.

### 3. Print & Printmaking
- **`risograph`** (Risograph Print): Overlapping translucent soy inks, misregistration jitter, dither dots.
- **`halftone-dossier`** (Halftone Dossier): Noir typewriter fonts, high-contrast newspaper dots, paperclips.
- **`woodcut`** (Woodcut Print): Chiseled gouge marks, intense black relief printing.

### 4. Edit & Documentary
- **`city-symphony`** (Time-lapse Symphony): Accelerated urban rhythms, light trails, montage cuts.
- **`visual-investigation`** (Visual Investigation): Synchronized forensic cameras, spatial maps, verified clocks.
- **`kino-montage`** (Constructivist Montage): Soviet avant-garde bold diagonals, typography, split frames.
- **`newsreel`** (1920s Newsreel): Film scratches, gate weave, flicker, silent intertitles.

### 5. Evidence Films
- **`satellite-briefing`** (Satellite Briefing): Multi-spectral false color, orbital telemetry, resolution scales.
- **`lidar-survey`** (LiDAR Survey): Millions of point-cloud dots, elevation color ramps, fly-through terrain.
- **`white-model`** (Projection on White Model): Architectural physical model mapped with projected light.
- **`teardown`** (Exploded Teardown): Precision mechanical components floating apart along guide rails.

### 6. Games & 3D
- **`hd-2d`** (HD-2D): 16-bit pixel sprites placed inside a modern 3D depth-of-field environment with volumetric fog.
- **`pixel-rpg`** (Pixel RPG): Nostalgic SNES color ramps, tile-based maps, sprite walk cycles.
- **`lowpoly-island`** (Low-poly 3D): Flat-shaded geometric meshes, ambient occlusion, stylized water.

## 💡 Prompt Synthesis Protocol

To synthesize a high-impact director prompt for an AI agent:
1. Load style rules from `opus-motion/data/styles.json`.
2. Anchor to the specific visual laws: prohibited techniques, required color palette, lighting rules.
3. Formulate the temporal timeline: state breakdown per beat, camera transition types, particle count.
4. Inject sound and voiceover cues with second-accurate markers `[t=0.0s..15.0s]`.
